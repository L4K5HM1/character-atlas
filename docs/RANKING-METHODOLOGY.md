# Ranking methodology

## What the score means

Scores are editorial, ordinal estimates of a **named form's general combat capability within its own series**. They are not official statistics, measured energy, fan polling, or fight probabilities. A score of 80 does not mean twice the power of 40. Gaps of a few points are interpretive; ties avoid false precision where the story does not establish a clear order.

Compare direct demonstrated feats first, then explicit story portrayal, then versatility and constraints. Do not treat job titles, bounties, age, or one conditional victory as automatic proof of superiority. Distinguish base, transformed, temporary, injured and prime states. No borrowed army, hypothetical transformation or unseen prime is assumed unless named.

The labels Developing (<55), Established (55–69), Advanced (70–84), Elite (85–94), and Exceptional (95–100) describe bands in this dataset, not canonical ranks. Exceptional is relative to the **included roster**, not proof that every possible character has been evaluated.

## Continuity boundaries

| Series | Scope | Excluded |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bleach | Original manga through chapter 686 | Novels, later one-shot, anime-only power additions |
| Naruto | Original manga through chapter 700 | Boruto, game-only abilities |
| One Piece | Manga through chapter 1057, end of Wano | Post-Wano fights and revelations |

The default chooses the strongest **listed** form. The roster is curated, not exhaustive: for example, Kenpachi's Bankai and Kakashi's temporary dual Mangekyō are not yet separate entries. Support abilities, especially Orihime's, cannot be captured well by a combat index.

## Rescaling decisions

- Shunsui's Bankai now exceeds his earlier Shikai. Base Guy no longer receives a score appropriate to an empowered form.
- Hashirama is below the Ten-Tails forms; war-arc Six Paths Naruto and Rinnegan Sasuke share a benchmark.
- Prime Roger and prime Whitebeard share a benchmark. Old Rayleigh and living Itachi are not hypothetical prime versions.
- Gear 5 Luffy and Kaido share a band. The Wano outcome involved accumulated damage and unusual conditions, so a narrow numerical winner would overstate the evidence.
- Ulquiorra's Segunda Etapa and Starrk are tied rather than presenting a disputed Espada comparison as settled.
- Corrected Minato's Kurama Chakra Mode label, Big Mom's Wano enhancement, Rukia's Shikai arc, and Uryū's manga-specific Antithesis entry.
- Removed unverified substats, unsupported bounties, and the old claims of official numbers or fan consensus. The overall estimate is not calculated from the removed attributes.

## Sources and limits of verification

The research pass checked official summaries for key benchmarks and form context. It did **not** verify every panel of all three manga or establish a consensus ranking. The score attached to every form remains an editorial inference. Card-level reading links locate the series; they do not claim to prove a numerical value.

- [Bleach episode 10 official summary](https://bleach-anime.com/en/story/?id=10): context for Kenpachi's training with Unohana and his earlier limitations. Used only where the adaptation corresponds to the manga.
- [VIZ Bleach volume 73](https://www.viz.com/manga-books/manga/bleach-volume-73-0/product/5544): Urahara's Bankai and the late-war confrontation.
- [VIZ Bleach volume 74](https://www.viz.com/manga-books/manga/bleach-volume-74-0/product/5664): Ichigo and his allies in the final Yhwach battle; a team outcome should not be read as a clean solo comparison.
- [Official Naruto finale retrospective](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1355): distinguishes the Ten-Tails transformation, Naruto and Sasuke's Hagoromo upgrades, and the final coordinated confrontation.
- [Official Flying Raijin explanation](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1709): supports Minato's teleportation mechanics; its Boruto comparisons are not used for scaling this roster.
- [Official One Piece episode 1076 listing](https://one-piece.com/anime/63846/index.html): Gear 5 Luffy and Kaido's Wano showdown.
- [Official One Piece episode 966 listing](https://one-piece.com/anime/o5691/index.html): Roger, Whitebeard and the Oden flashback context. These episode listings support context, not precise numeric comparisons.

Primary reading indexes: [Bleach](https://www.viz.com/shonenjump/chapters/bleach), [Naruto](https://www.viz.com/shonenjump/chapters/naruto), [One Piece](https://www.viz.com/shonenjump/chapters/one-piece). Some chapters require a subscription.

## Propose a correction

Open an issue with the character ID, exact form, chapter or episode reference, the relevant conditions, and the proposed comparison. Explain both why the old placement fails and what the new one should be. Update the form note alongside its score. Automated ranking tests protect intended relationships but cannot determine canon truth.
