How the rankings work

Each form is placed on its own series’ relative 0–100 index. Higher means stronger general combat capability under the named conditions. The scale is ordinal: 80 is not twice as strong as 40. Close scores are debatable; ties are intentional.

Strongest listed forms appear first. Some later or temporary forms are not included. Support specialists may contribute much more than this combat-focused index suggests.

Unverified databook totals, invented Haki percentages, and the old universal ranking have been removed. Read the methodology and source notes.
